Privacy Policy

App Name: Gold Saving Book (com.goldapp.simulate)
Effective Date: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gold Saving Book ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile application package com.goldapp.simulate.

1. Local On-Device Data Storage

Your personal gold transactions and simulation data remain on your device.

We believe in privacy by design. All personal gold holdings, transaction records, purchase notes, and custom simulation setups are saved strictly in your device's local memory (SharedPreferences/device storage). We do not collect, store, or transmit your transaction logs to external servers.

2. Information Collected by Third-Party SDKs

While we do not collect personal identifiers on our own servers, the App integrates trusted third-party Google SDKs to support app functionality, analytics, and advertising:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Used to serve non-intrusive advertisements. AdMob may collect and process Advertising ID (AD_ID), device identifiers, IP addresses, and app interaction diagnostics to serve and measure ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services & In-App Reviews: Used to manage app installation, updates, and in-app rating dialogs directly through the Google Play Store.

3. Use of Advertising ID (AD_ID)

The App declares the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission required by Google Mobile Ads SDK for serving ads and preventing advertising fraud. Users can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time via their Android device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

4. Data Security

Because your transaction data is stored locally on your device, its security depends on your device's security controls. We recommend protecting your device with a PIN, pattern, or biometric lock.

5. Children's Privacy

Gold Saving Book is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated Effective Date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@goldsavingbook.app
